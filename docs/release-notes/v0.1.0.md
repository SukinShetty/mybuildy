## MyBuildy 0.1.0 — your AI coding agent, explained

MyBuildy is a desktop companion for people who build software with an AI coding agent but don't read code. It watches your coding agent's window, tells you in plain English what just happened, checks it against what you said you wanted to build, keeps track of what's done and what's missing, and gives you the exact next prompt to type. A small robot on your screen reads it out loud. You stay in charge: MyBuildy suggests, you approve each step.

### What's included

- **Watching and explaining.** Pick your coding agent's window once. After each turn MyBuildy says what happened, what it means for your project, what's built, missing or broken, and where you seem stuck.
- **The next prompt, ready to go.** Copy it, or click **Paste into terminal** to put it into the exact window you picked (Windows and macOS). If that window has closed, nothing is pasted and you are told so; the prompt stays on your clipboard. MyBuildy never presses Enter.
- **Goal tracking.** Say what you're building and when it counts as done. MyBuildy checks each step against that goal and tells you when you're there.
- **Guided first-run setup.** One step per screen: your AI key, a model that passes a quick "can it see your screen?" check, your goal, and your coding agent.
- **A local model, no key needed.** In setup, choose **Use a local model instead (Ollama or LM Studio)**. MyBuildy finds the app on your computer, lists your models with the ones that can read images first, and says what to do if the app isn't running or you have no model that can read images. Screenshots and memory then stay on your computer.
- **Recommended models only.** For Anthropic, OpenAI and OpenRouter you see a short list of tested vision models (with "Show all" for advanced users), and clear, plain-English messages with a next step when something goes wrong.
- **Voice.** Spoken guidance in Buildy's own voice, which runs on your computer, free, with nothing sent anywhere: choose **Female voice** (the default) or **Male voice** in Settings → Voice. Optional: ElevenLabs with your own key, which also adds a click-to-talk microphone for questions. Your computer's voice speaks only if Buildy's voice can't run.
- **Per-project memory**, kept on your computer only. Your API keys are stored encrypted.

### Install

**Windows**
1. Download `MyBuildy-Setup-0.1.0.exe`.
2. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns you. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac**
1. Download `MyBuildy-0.1.0-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon (M1 and later) or `MyBuildy-0.1.0-x64.dmg` for Intel. (Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means arm64.)
2. Open the DMG and drag **MyBuildy** into **Applications**, then open it. The app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally.
3. When asked, allow **Screen Recording** (so MyBuildy can see your coding agent) and, if you want Paste into terminal, **Accessibility**.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` on this page lets you verify each download.

### What you need

- **A coding agent** running in a terminal window, such as Claude Code. MyBuildy watches any agent; Paste into terminal is built and tested against Claude Code.
- **An AI key with credits** from Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter. Your key needs credits on that provider's account: ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro subscriptions don't include these. MyBuildy sends screenshots of the watched window to the provider you choose, and only to that provider.
- **Or a local model:** Ollama or LM Studio on your computer with a model that can read images (a vision model). No key, no per-call cost; your computer needs to be powerful enough to run it.

### Known limitations

- **Google Gemini** is available under Advanced but is **not yet tested**.
- **The Windows installer is unsigned**, so SmartScreen asks once.
- **macOS is new in this release**: tested in CI, and first hands-on testing on real Macs is under way. Reports welcome.
- **Linux** runs from source only and is untested.
- **The destructive-prompt guard is a speed bump, not a guarantee.** You remain the final check before anything runs.
- **MyBuildy judges from screenshots.** It sees what's on screen, not what happened inside your code, so it can be wrong when the screen doesn't tell the whole story.
